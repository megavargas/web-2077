## Which cookies we use

This website only uses **technical cookies** that are necessary for it to work. We do not use analytics, advertising or third-party cookies, so your consent is not required.

| Cookie            | Purpose                                  | Duration |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------- | -------- |
| `i18n_redirected` | Remembering the language you have chosen | 1 year   |

## How to disable them

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. If you block the language cookie, the site will still work but will not remember your choice.
